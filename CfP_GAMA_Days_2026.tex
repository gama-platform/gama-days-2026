% Introduction
\noindent Following several successful editions, the GAMA Days 2026 is the international conference where users and developers of the GAMA modelling and simulation platform meet to present work, discuss difficulties, and collaborate on exciting topics.

% Submission Content
\section*{Abstract Submission}
GAMA Days 2026 seeks submissions of abstracts that: 
\begin{itemize}[label=\color{gamaOrange}$\bullet$]
    \item Present a \textbf{new feature} for GAMA. 
    \item Present a \textbf{model} that uses GAMA. 
    \item Propose a \textbf{live demo} or \textbf{master class}. 
\end{itemize}

\noindent At least one author of each submission is required to register, attend, and present at the conference.
